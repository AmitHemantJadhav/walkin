What it does

A house you can talk to.

01

Ask John anything

Say "Hey John" and ask. How big is this room, where does the light come from, what's the listing price. He answers out loud, in about a second.

02

"What would look good here?"

John furnishes the room in a style that suits the house, every piece at its real size, with a price tag floating on it and the room total overhead. Give him a budget and he makes it fit.

03

Change the walls

Point at a wall and say "make this brick". Drop in a plant that suits the light. Pick up the sofa with your hands and see if it fits through the door.

04

Put it on your face

Your iPhone becomes the headset: a 3D-printed viewer with standard Cardboard lenses, and the house in stereo all around you.
